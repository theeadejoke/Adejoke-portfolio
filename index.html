03 / A little about me

Make it human.
Make it matter.

The best work starts with paying attention—to people, culture, and the small details that make a voice feel like itself.

I’m Adejoke Adekunle Alagba, a creative strategist, writer, and brand storyteller with over three years of experience. I help brands find their voice, communicate their value, and connect with the people they want to reach.

My work spans Nigerian financial education, music, events, and literary publishing. I’m also a published poet; emotional precision and cultural specificity shape both my creative and brand work.
